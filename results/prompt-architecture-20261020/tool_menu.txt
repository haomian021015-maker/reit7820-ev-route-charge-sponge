AGENT charging (smac-charging-tripfeasibility) - EV Charging
  geocode_place: Convert a South East Queensland place name or address into WGS84 coordinates.

Use this before any tool that needs a lat/lon when you were given a place name
instead of numbers. Do not guess coordinates - a recalled coordinate has no
source and cannot be cited. Returns the matched address and the OpenStreetMap
feature type, so you can judge how precise the match is.

Covers South East Queensland; a place resolving outside SEQ is reported as out
of scope. Returns the single best match and does not disambiguate between
places sharing a name.
      place: Place name, landmark, suburb or address to locate, e.g. 'Toowong Village Brisbane'.
  find_nearest_charger: Find the closest Queensland Electric Super Highway charging stations to a point.

Answers range-planning questions for electric vehicle trips starting in South
East Queensland - where the next public charging site is, how far away, and
which sites lie onward along the highway network. Uses the Queensland
Government's published station list, so it covers intercity corridors rather
than every kerbside charger in a suburb.

Origin must be in South East Queensland; returned stations may be anywhere in
Queensland, since onward stations matter on long trips. Plug and connector
types are NOT available - the source publishes that column empty. Does not
report live availability or pricing, and does not plan a route.
      lat: WGS84 latitude of the trip origin, in South East Queensland.
      lon: WGS84 longitude of the trip origin, in South East Queensland.
      max_results: How many of the closest stations to return, 1 to 5.
  assess_ev_trip_feasibility: Decide whether an electric vehicle can finish a trip on the range it has left, and where to charge if it cannot.

Takes an origin, a destination and the range remaining in the battery. Estimates
the road distance, holds back a safety reserve, and returns one of three verdicts:
`feasible` (drive straight there), `requires_charging` (a stop is needed) or
`infeasible` (no public charger is reachable on the range left). When a stop is
needed, public chargers that are reachable and close to the direct line are ranked
by the time they add to the trip - the detour plus the charging needed to finish -
and each reports its rated power, connector types, the range and energy to add,
and whether a full charge there completes the trip. An optional connector type
limits the stops to chargers the vehicle can use.

The origin must be in South East Queensland; the destination may be anywhere in
Queensland. Stations come from a pinned Open Charge Map snapshot of Queensland,
dated in every response, cross-checked against the Queensland Government's
Electric Super Highway list, which flags the state's designated corridor sites.

Takes coordinates, not place names: call `geocode_place` first. Road distance is
straight-line distance x 1.3, not a routed path. Charging time is a minimum: it
assumes the charger's rated power, capped at the vehicle's limit if one is given,
and charging slows past about 80%. Does NOT report live availability or
occupancy - no open source publishes it, and most station records were last
verified years ago - nor pricing. Plans a single stop, not a multi-stop itinerary.
      origin_lat: WGS84 latitude of where the trip starts. Must be in South East Queensland.
      origin_lon: WGS84 longitude of where the trip starts. Must be in South East Queensland.
      dest_lat: WGS84 latitude of the destination. May be anywhere in Queensland.
      dest_lon: WGS84 longitude of the destination. May be anywhere in Queensland.
      remaining_range_km: Range left in the battery right now, in kilometres, as the vehicle reports it. Must be greater than zero.
      full_range_km: Range in kilometres on a full battery. Optional: the verdict and the recommended stops do not need it. Supply it to learn whether one stop is enough to finish the trip (completes_trip_after_stop), and to cap the charge a stop asks for at a full battery.
      connector_type: Only recommend chargers that have this connector. Optional; omit it to consider every connector. CCS2 and CHAdeMO are DC fast-charging plugs; Type2 is the AC plug most Australian EVs use.
      consumption_kwh_per_100km: The vehicle's energy use in kWh per 100 km, which turns the range a stop must add into energy and so into charging time. Defaults to 18, typical of a mid-size EV.
      vehicle_max_charge_kw: The most power the vehicle can accept while charging, in kW. Optional; without it each charger's full rated power is assumed, so charging times are minimums and the most powerful chargers rank first even for a car that cannot use that power.
      safety_margin_pct: Percentage of range held back as a reserve rather than planned for, 0 to 50. Defaults to 10.
      max_results: How many charging stops to return when the trip needs one, 1 to 5.
      explain: Include a short written explanation of the verdict alongside the computed figures.
